# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

CJ Little and Jeff Parker are equal primary users. They prepare Nonsense podcast stories at a desktop before recording, often under time pressure and after substantial independent research. Their job is to turn topics, links, rough notes, current reporting, and earlier show coverage into a focused conversation plan without outsourcing editorial judgment or their deliberation.

## Product Purpose

The Nonsense Research Harness is a private conversational research workbench. One bounded agent answers ordinary questions, researches supplied and current sources, searches prior Nonsense transcripts, and retains useful evidence for follow-ups. When CJ or Jeff explicitly asks, it can also create a validated, cited Headlines or Big Story preparation brief ranked by editorial priority. Success means either host can get to trustworthy evidence and a clear synthesis faster without outsourcing editorial judgment, order, cuts, or episode timing.

## Positioning

A podcast research agent that understands the hosts' real preparation format and earlier conversations while leaving topic selection, argument, humor, writing, and final editorial control to them.

## Brand Personality

Focused, candid, and workmanlike. The interface should feel like a trusted studio workbench: dense enough for serious preparation, calm enough for long sessions, and explicit about what is complete, partial, unavailable, or unverified.

## Anti-references

Do not make a generic chatbot, a decorative AI dashboard, a marketing-style SaaS surface, or an interface that turns hidden reasoning into theater. Avoid fake workspace history, invented sources, oversized metrics, glass effects, cyberpunk styling, excessive cards, and visual polish that competes with the artifact.

## Design Principles

- Make the continuing research conversation the primary object; a preparation brief is an optional output.
- Show concise observable work, evidence, limitations, and cost without exposing private reasoning or raw provider internals.
- Lead ordinary answers with the conclusion and place readable citations beside the claims they support.
- Keep research depth separate from output format: Deep research never silently creates a brief.
- Preserve CJ and Jeff's productive deliberation instead of choosing stories or scripting host dialogue.
- Prefer familiar controls and restrained hierarchy over novel interaction for its own sake.
- Fail honestly and preserve useful work when a source, model, budget, or session is unavailable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and semantics. Support complete keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader labels and live announcements, reduced motion, 44-pixel touch targets on coarse pointers, responsive use from narrow mobile screens through large desktops, and error language that explains what happened and what the user can do next.
